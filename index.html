How a job runs

Most of a good finish happens before the lid comes off the tin. Here's what to expect from the first visit to the last walk-round.

  1. 1

    Visit and quote

    We come out, look at the job, talk through colours and finishes, and give you a clear price.

  2. 2

    Protect and prep

    Floors and furniture sheeted, windows and edges masked, then filling and dust-free sanding.

  3. 3

    Paint or spray

    Brush, roller or airless sprayer, whichever gives the best result on that surface.

  4. 4

    Tidy and walk-round

    Everything cleared away, then we walk the job with you before calling it finished.
